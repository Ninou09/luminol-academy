# Public flagship redesign — audit, direction and asset governance

## Phase 1: audit

The previous public experience had a strong technical base: governed routes,
route-specific metadata, working enquiries, Sanity programme content, responsive
navigation, reduced-motion handling and production security headers.

The visual weaknesses were:

- repeated orbit, circle and oversized-letter graphics across too many sections
- a hero that consumed too much of a laptop viewport
- continuous floating, spinning and marquee motion
- generic or unverifiable statistics
- no governed display of approved team profiles or testimonials
- programme cards that looked too similar across the three schools
- a premium homepage followed by internal pages using an older visual language
- a static `EN · AR` label without an actual approved Arabic content experience
- insufficient human photography and institutional evidence
- small utility text and weak contrast in some dark sections

Credibility was damaged most by repeated abstract visuals and proof-like claims
that were not sourced from governed content.

## What was preserved

- all public routes
- Sanity programme integration and safe fallbacks
- enquiry form and API behavior
- metadata, canonical URLs and Open Graph URLs
- sitemap and robots behavior
- authentication and database boundaries outside the public redesign
- certificate verification routes
- security headers
- progressive enhancement and reduced-motion support

## Phase 2: creative direction

### Concept

**The connected academy.**

The visual system combines editorial education design with human photography,
disciplined typography and branch-specific accents. The homepage introduces one
academy, then gives Psychology, Languages and Professional Training distinct
moods without fragmenting the master brand.

### Principles

1. Human evidence before decorative spectacle.
2. Typography and spacing create hierarchy; cards are secondary.
3. Every section has one job and one conversion path.
4. The three schools are distinct in mood, not presented as unrelated brands.
5. Proof is published only when it is approved and governed.
6. Motion is restrained and never required for comprehension.
7. Mobile composition is deliberately restructured.
8. Arabic direction and typography are built into the system, while public
   Arabic copy waits for approved content rather than using poor placeholder
   translation.

### Reference translation

The education references informed the use of clear utility navigation,
institutional mission, programme discovery, recent opportunities and proof.
Their layouts, brand assets, copy and code were not copied.

## Phase 3: implementation scope

- Rebuilt the homepage around a compact photo-led hero.
- Added immediate navigation to the three schools.
- Replaced fake statistics and the marquee with credibility principles.
- Reduced six repetitive programme cards to a focused programme index.
- Added institutional story, expertise standards, governed team profiles and
  governed consent-confirmed testimonials.
- Added learning opportunities without inventing dates.
- Rebuilt About and Contact using the same editorial system.
- Rebuilt all three dynamic school pages with branch-specific photography,
  outcomes, method, audiences, expertise, governed evidence, FAQs and specific
  calls to action.
- Added licensed remote-image support.
- Added structured EducationalOrganization data.
- Refined the header, navigation, mobile menu and footer.
- Reduced animation distance and duration.
- Expanded the documented design system.

## Missing approved assets and content

The following items cannot be fabricated and remain content-production tasks:

- official production logo artwork
- approved founder portrait
- authentic classroom, workshop and psychology-session photography
- approved instructor biographies and portraits
- consent-confirmed learner testimonials
- confirmed event dates and registration details
- complete, professionally reviewed Arabic copy for every public route
- verified institutional statistics or outcomes suitable for public display

The implementation safely hides empty governed team and testimonial sections
rather than publishing fake content.

## Replacement photography shot list

- academy entrance and classroom establishing photographs
- founder portrait in horizontal and vertical crops
- one real image for each branch
- instructor portraits with consistent lighting and background treatment
- active language conversation
- facilitated professional workshop
- approved psychology educational/support setting
- certificates, learning materials and detail photographs
- group images with publication permission

Each image should be assigned a page, crop ratio, alt text and publication
permission before upload.

## Final design-director review criteria

- The hero fits a common laptop viewport and exposes the school navigator.
- No major section relies on animation.
- Every public page uses the flagship system.
- No fake testimonial, statistic, event or instructor appears.
- Branch personality is visible through content, image and accent—not gimmicks.
- Forms, navigation and calls to action remain keyboard accessible.
- Mobile layouts preserve hierarchy rather than shrinking desktop layouts.
- Arabic-ready typography and logical CSS are present; unapproved translation is
  not published.
