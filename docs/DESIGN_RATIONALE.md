# Luminol Academy — Design Rationale

> **"Knowledge that glows."**
> Luminol is the substance that emits a soft blue light. The redesign turns that
> name into a visual language: every page is a journey from darkness toward a
> gentle, guiding light — which is exactly what good psychology, learning and
> personal growth feel like from the inside.

---

## 1. The core idea: an inner universe

The website is designed as **a journey through the inner world of a person**.

| What you see                            | What it means                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stars**                               | Individual thoughts — countless, quiet, each one a small light.                    |
| **Nebulae** (soft coloured clouds)      | Emotions — they have no hard edges, they drift and change colour.                  |
| **Glass forms drifting past**           | Ideas — clear, fragile, catching light from different angles.                      |
| **Constellations that draw themselves** | Connections between thoughts — meaning appears when points are linked.             |
| **Glowing nerve fibres with pulses**    | Signals of the nervous system — the mind is alive and always communicating.        |
| **Pearl crystal rocks**                 | Inner strength and resilience — solid, yet full of colour when light touches them. |
| **The ringed planet at the end**        | **The Self** — the destination of every journey of growth.                         |

Scrolling moves the camera _deeper inward_. The visitor literally travels
through the mind while reading, which mirrors what happens in therapy or
learning: you go deeper, and things become clearer.

---

## 2. Why a sphere (the circle)

The sphere is the central symbol of the brand, used in the hero, the breathing
exercise, the "One human journey" orbit and the programme cards.

- **Wholeness.** A circle has no beginning and no end. Luminol works with the
  _whole person_ — mind, voice and career — not one isolated skill.
- **Safety.** Round forms have no sharp corners; the brain reads them as calm
  and non-threatening. This matters for people arriving at a psychology
  service, often in a vulnerable moment.
- **Protection and containment.** A glass sphere _holds_ something precious
  inside it — like a therapeutic space holds a person's story with care.
- **Transparency.** The sphere is made of clear glass: nothing hidden, an
  honest and open institution.
- **A cell, a planet, an eye.** The same shape appears at every scale of life,
  from a single neuron to a world — growth happens at every scale.

### The brain inside the sphere

The hero shows a luminous brain held inside a glass sphere: **the mind,
protected and illuminated**. It says, in one image, what Luminol offers —
scientific understanding of the mind, delivered with care.

---

## 3. Why a drop / liquid light

The glowing core in the "Three schools, one light" scene moves like a liquid
drop, and light inside the hero sphere flows like water.

- **Luminol itself is a liquid** that glows — the name made visible.
- **Change is fluid.** People do not transform in hard steps; feelings and
  habits shift gradually, like liquid finding a new shape.
- **A single drop creates ripples.** One conversation, one course, one
  consultation spreads outward into family, work and community.
- **Tears and relief.** In psychology, a drop is also emotion released — the
  moment something heavy begins to lighten.

---

## 4. One light, three schools (the orbit)

On the home page a single glowing core releases three glass spheres onto their
orbits; on the About page they circle around the central "Luminol" light.

- **One source** — Luminol's shared values and founder-led expertise.
- **Three orbits** — Psychology (teal), Languages (blue), Professional
  Development (bronze-gold).
- They **move independently but never leave the same centre**: each school has
  its own path, yet they are connected by one gravity — the growth of the
  whole person.
- Beams of light rise from each school to the core: every programme leads back
  to the same purpose.

---

## 5. The brain made of neurons — "Inside the mind"

Eleven thousand points of light gather from the stars into a brain, then begin
to "think": waves of activity cross the cortex and impulses race along its
connections. Three labels appear: **Thoughts · Feelings · Actions**.

This is the **cognitive triangle**, a foundation of modern psychology:
what we think influences what we feel, which influences what we do — and
each one can change the others. The animation shows the mind as a _living
network_, not a fixed object: connections can be built, strengthened and
re-wired. That is the message of hope behind every Luminol programme.

---

## 6. The breathing space

A real, interactive breathing exercise (4 seconds in, 4 hold, 6 out — the
longer exhale calms the nervous system). The sphere grows and softens with the
breath.

It turns the website from something you _read_ into something that _helps
you right now_, in one minute. It shows the academy's care before any form is
filled in. It is clearly labelled as a gentle exercise, not a substitute for
professional care.

---

## 7. The living inkblot

Near the final call to action, soft mirror-symmetric "ink" slowly folds and
unfolds — a respectful nod to the Rorschach plates, one of the most recognised
symbols of psychology. It is watercolour-soft on purpose: curiosity and
self-reflection, not clinical testing.

## 9. Colour and material language

- **Deep night blue** — calm, depth, trust; the space where reflection happens.
- **Luminol cyan** — clarity, insight, the "glow" of understanding.
- **Gold** — warmth, humanity and value; used for numbers, labels and the
  final "Luminol" wordmark.
- **Pearl and white** — honesty and lightness, so the dark theme never feels
  heavy.
- **Glass** everywhere — transparency, fragility handled with care, and light
  passing _through_ people rather than hiding them.
- **Glass human figures without faces** — inclusive (anyone can see
  themselves in them), respectful of privacy, and honest: they are clearly
  illustrations, never pretending to be real clients.

The footer wordmark constantly flows from silver to white to gold: **light is
never static — learning never stops.**

---

## 10. Motion principles

- **Slow and weighty, never nervous.** Everything eases in; nothing jumps.
  Calm motion is part of the psychological message.
- **Motion follows the reader.** Scrolling drives depth, the pointer gently
  tilts cards and steers the 3D scenes — the visitor is always in control.
- **Accessible by design.** People who enable "reduced motion" get a calm,
  static version with all content intact; touch devices get lighter effects;
  Arabic (right-to-left) layouts are mirrored correctly.
- **Content first.** No original text, page or credit was removed — the design
  only adds light around it.

---

## 11. Technology (for the technical team)

- **Three.js** (MIT) — all 3D scenes, custom GLSL shaders (stars, nebulae,
  iridescent crystals, neuron brain, curl-noise particle flow on the GPU).
- **Official three.js add-ons** (MIT) — bloom, film grain, vignette and lens
  fringing for the cinematic finish.
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SplitText** — scroll choreography and text reveals.
- **Lenis** — smooth scrolling on desktop.
- All heavy scenes load lazily, pause when off-screen or when the tab is hidden,
  and fall back gracefully when WebGL is unavailable.
